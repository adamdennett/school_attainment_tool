## Verdict: `JEP_Paper_tight.qmd` is NOT the submitted version.

The submitted PDF was rendered by the journal (Research Papers in Education /
RRED, ScholarOne) from **`Manuscript_Anonymous.docx`**. That docx was **hand-edited
and tightened after the last render of the qmd**, so the qmd is stale.

- **27% of body sentences (80 of 299) in the submitted docx have no close match
  in the qmd.** The divergence is throughout, not localised.
- **Abstract fully rewritten.** Docx: *"The UK government's 2026 White Paper
  targets halving the disadvantage attainment gap. Achieving this requires…"* —
  much tighter than the qmd's *"In its 2026 White Paper, the UK government has set
  a worthwhile and ambitious target to halve…"*.

- **§5.1 Local context edited** — e.g. the transport passage: docx merges to a
  single *"approximately £606,000 already spent"* in §5.1; the qmd still has the
  itemised *"£339,000 … a further £267,000 … around 600 bus passes"* there. The
  third council objective and the "more mixed pupil intake" design-principle

- **Literature review tightened** in places.

### What did NOT change

The **model outputs are consistent** between the two: §5.2 reads
*"7th out of 152 … +0.06 … 6% uplift … 2 GCSE points"* identically in both. So
the divergence is **prose tightening only**, not re-analysis. Figures, tables and
numbers still correspond to the same model runs.

## Implication for the revision

- The **reviewers read the docx text**, so all point-by-point responses and prose
  edits must be made against the **docx**, not the stale qmd.
- But the revision **requires new model output** (R2.3 KS2 robustness, R2.4 Ofsted
  fixed effect, R2.7 with/without-absence caterpillar) — new tables and figures
  that can only be produced from the **qmd pipeline**. You cannot regenerate these
  by hand-editing a docx.

So both files are needed, and they must be reconciled before editing.

## Recommended path

1. **Back-port the docx's prose edits into `JEP_Paper_tight.qmd`** so the qmd once
   again reproduces the submitted text. Commit that as a clean "as-submitted"
   baseline. (Prose-merge only — no re-analysis, since numbers already match.)
2. **Then apply the revision edits to the qmd** and re-render, so the pipeline
   stays the single source of truth and the new model tables/figures regenerate
   correctly.
3. **Produce the tracked-changes docx** for the journal from the revised render at
   the end (most journals want changes marked on the manuscript file).

Alternative (not recommended): edit the docx directly with tracked changes and
hand-paste the new tables. Faster for prose but the new figures/tables can't be
regenerated by hand, and the qmd pipeline would rot further.
